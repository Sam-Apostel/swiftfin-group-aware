The fin — how it moves through the app
1Swims off the edge. Always cropped by a screen or container edge, heading somewhere. Never centred like a logo inside the app.
2One per screen. Ambient at 10–20% behind chrome; full strength only when it is the content (welcome, couch picker, empty states).
3It's alive. Loading = a fin gently swimming in place. Pull to refresh = a flick of the tail.
×Never on top of posters, artwork or video. When something plays, the fin is gone.
